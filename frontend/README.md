# 🔬 AutoResearch AI — Research Paper Analysis System

AutoResearch AI is an AI-powered research assistant designed to help researchers analyze academic literature, identify research trends, discover research gaps, verify evidence, and generate structured literature reviews and research reports.

This repository contains the **Streamlit frontend** of the AutoResearch AI project.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of AutoResearch AI is to simplify the academic research process by providing an intelligent interface for:

- Academic paper discovery
- Research paper analysis
- Evidence verification
- Research trend analysis
- Research gap identification
- Literature review generation
- Research report generation

The system is being developed as a **B.Tech final-year project**.

---
